Dumpling

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • Dumpling wrappers
  • Ground pork or chicken
  • Napa cabbage (shredded)
  • Soy sauce & sesame oil
  • Ginger & garlic (minced)

Steps:

  1. Mix minced protein base arrays uniformly with garden cabbage strips and soy infusions.
  2. Spoon small raw savory filling spheres into central wrapper layout rings.
  3. Moisten wrapper edge boundaries gently using clean wet finger points.
  4. Fold wrapper membranes over core fills, pleating structural walls securely shut.
  5. Pan-fry bases crisp golden, add water sheets, and steam-trap beneath lids until tender.

Mapo Tofu

⏱️ 20 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 block silken tofu (cubed)
  • 100g minced pork or beef
  • 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (ground)
  • Dashi or chicken stock

Steps:

  1. Sauté ground seasoned meat blends raw inside hot wok metal layouts.
  2. Incorporate rich spicy doubanjiang oil compounds until bases run red.
  3. Pour fluid broth volumes slow, bringing sauce matrices to mild bubbling.
  4. Slide fragile silken tofu cuboid matrix units carefully into hot liquids.
  5. Thicken via clear starch slurry additions, then dust raw buzzing peppercorn powder on top.
