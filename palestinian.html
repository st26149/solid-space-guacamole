Falafel

⏱️ 25 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas (soaked overnight, do not use canned)
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley & 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1 small onion (chopped) & 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin & 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Oil for deep frying

Steps:

  1. Add the thoroughly drained soaked chickpeas, herbs, onion, and garlic to a food processor.
  2. Pulse the ingredients until finely minced, resembling coarse sand (do not puree).
  3. Transfer mixture to a bowl, stir in cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper. Refrigerate for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir the baking soda into the cold mixture right before shaping.
  5. Form the mixture into small balls or patties using wet hands or a scoop tool.
  6. Deep fry in hot oil until deep golden brown and crunchy on the outside (around 4-5 minutes).

Musakhan

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 4 chicken pieces (bone-in, skin-on)
  • 3 large onions (finely diced or sliced)
  • 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp ground sumac spice
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice & cumin
  • Taboon or flatbread, toasted pine nuts

Steps:

  1. Rub chicken pieces with olive oil, half the sumac, allspice, salt, and pepper. Roast till fully done.
  2. While chicken roasts, cook sliced onions in a large pool of olive oil on low heat until ultra soft.
  3. Stir the remaining sumac into the soft caramelized sweet onions until deep dark purple.
  4. Dip pieces of flatbread directly into the warm sumac onion oil matrix to coat.
  5. Spread the purple sumac onions over the bread, then top with roasted chicken pieces.
  6. Broil the assembled bread for 3 minutes until edges crisp, then cover with toasted pine nuts.
