Doro Wat

⏱️ 75 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Chicken pieces (skinned)
  • 3 large Red onions (pureed or minced fine)
  • 3 tbsp Niter Kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter)
  • 2-3 tbsp Berbere spice blend
  • 4 Hard-boiled eggs
  • 1 lemon (juiced)
  • Salt and water as needed

Steps:

  1. Marinate skinned chicken pieces in fresh lemon juice and salt for about 15 minutes, then drain.
  2. Simmer minced onions in a dry pan without oil or butter for 20-30 minutes until completely reduced and dry.
  3. Add Niter Kibbeh butter and Berbere spices to the onions. Fry together on low heat until highly fragrant.
  4. Add chicken pieces and a splash of water. Cover and simmer gently for 35 minutes until the chicken is perfectly tender.
  5. Pierce hard-boiled eggs with a fork and slide them into the dark red sauce. Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes before serving with Injera bread.

Misir Wot

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups Red lentils (rinsed)
  • 2 large Red onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Berbere spice blend
  • 3 tbsp Vegetable oil or vegan spiced butter
  • 2 cloves Garlic & 1 tsp Ginger (minced)
  • 2.5 cups Water or vegetable broth

Steps:

  1. Cook chopped onions dry in a saucepan over medium heat until sweet and translucent.
  2. Pour in oil and add Berbere spice blend, cooking down for about 5 minutes to release flavor layers.
  3. Stir in minced garlic and ginger, then fold in rinsed red lentils.
  4. Pour over your water or broth and bring to a soft boil.
  5. Lower heat, cover, and let simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are soft, creamy, and have absorbed the spiced sauce base.
