Ika Mata

⏱️ 20 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Fresh white fish fillets (Tuna or Snapper)
  • 4-5 Fresh lemons or limes (juiced)
  • 1 cup Thick coconut cream
  • 1 Cucumber (seeded and finely diced)
  • 1 Spring onion (finely sliced)
  • 1/2 Red bell pepper (diced)
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Cut your fresh white fish fillets into clean, bite-sized 1cm cubes.
  2. Place fish cubes into a bowl, pour over the fresh lemon/lime juice so it's fully submerged, and marinate in the fridge for 15-20 mins until opaque.
  3. Drain off most of the excess citrus juice from the bowl.
  4. Add your finely diced cucumber, spring onions, red bell pepper, and a generous pinch of salt to the fish.
  5. Pour the thick coconut cream over the ingredients, toss gently to combine, and serve thoroughly chilled.

Lu Vegetable

⏱️ 60 mins

Ingredients:

  • 15-20 Fresh taro leaves
  • 2 cups Spinach or silverbeet leaves (chopped)
  • 1 large Onion (diced)
  • 1 can Thick coconut cream
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
  • Aluminum foil wrap

Steps:

  1. Lay out a strong piece of aluminum foil and construct an overlapping base using fresh taro leaves.
  2. Fill the leaf center with chopped spinach, diced onions, salt, and pepper.
  3. Slowly pour your thick coconut cream over the mixed vegetables.
  4. Fold the taro leaves securely around the veggie mixture, then double-wrap with foil to ensure it stays sealed airtight.
  5. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for roughly 50 to 60 minutes until everything inside is melt-in-your-mouth tender.
