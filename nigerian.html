Jollof Chicken

⏱️ 60 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Chicken pieces (thighs or drumsticks)
  • 3 cups Long-grain parboiled rice
  • 1 can Tomato paste & 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 Red bell peppers & 1 Scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 large Onions (sliced)
  • 2 cups Chicken stock
  • Thyme, curry powder, and bay leaves

Steps:

  1. Blend fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet, and one onion into a smooth purée.
  2. Season and sear chicken pieces in oil until golden brown, then set aside.
  3. In the same oil, sauté sliced onions and tomato paste until darkened slightly. Pour in the blended purée and fry down until reduced.
  4. Stir in stock, curry, thyme, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then add washed parboiled rice.
  5. Nestle chicken back into the pot, cover tightly with foil and a lid, and steam over low heat for 30 minutes until the rice is tender.

Jollof Veg

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups Basmati or long-grain rice
  • 1 cup Mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, sweetcorn)
  • 1 blended Tomato and Pepper base mixture
  • 1 large Onion (diced)
  • 2 cups Vegetable stock
  • 3 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • Salt, seasoning cubes, and bay leaves

Steps:

  1. Fry diced onions in vegetable oil until translucent, then add seasoned pepper sauce base.
  2. Pour in vegetable stock, toss in bay leaves, and bring the sauce mix to a steady simmer.
  3. Stir in washed rice thoroughly so it gets coated in the red rich stew base.
  4. Cover securely and cook on low heat for 25 minutes.
  5. Fold in fresh mixed vegetables gently during the last 5 minutes of steaming to keep them bright and crisp before serving.
