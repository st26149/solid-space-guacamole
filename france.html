Coq au Vin

⏱️ 60 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 kg chicken (cut into portions)
  • 200g bacon lardons & 250g button mushrooms
  • 2 cloves garlic (chopped) & 2 big spoons butter
  • 750ml red wine (Burgundy)
  • 1 small spoon sugar, salt, and pepper

Steps:

  1. Melt butter in a large pan on medium heat and fry bacon until crisp.
  2. Add chicken pieces and brown them well on all sides.
  3. Stir in garlic, mushrooms, and sugar, cooking for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the red wine, cover, and let it simmer for 30–40 minutes until the chicken is tender.

Ratatouille

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 large eggplants & 3 zucchini (sliced)
  • 1 red bell pepper (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (chopped)
  • 1 cup tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • 3 spoons olive oil, salt, and herbs

Steps:

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat and sauté garlic and peppers.
  2. Layer the sliced eggplants and zucchini over the mixture.
  3. Pour the tomato sauce and herbs over the top layer.
  4. Cover and simmer on low-medium heat for 25 minutes until the vegetables are perfectly tender.
