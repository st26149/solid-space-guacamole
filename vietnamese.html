Pho Beef

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 150g narrow rice noodles
  • 4 cups clear spiced beef broth
  • 100g raw beef flank (shaved thin)
  • Star anise & cinnamon pods
  • Bean sprouts, basil & lime

Steps:

  1. Infuse commercial bone broths with roasted star anise and cinnamon.
  2. Boil delicate rice starch noodles separately and drain clear.
  3. Layer cold cooked noodles directly into deep porcelain bowls.
  4. Drape raw, translucent, paper-thin beef strips over noodles.
  5. Pour rolling, boiling-hot clear broth directly over the beef to flash cook it.
  6. Serve alongside platters of crisp green herbs and lime wedges.

Banh Mi Tofu

⏱️ 20 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 crusty French baguette
  • 150g firm tofu (sliced & fried)
  • Pickled daikon radish & carrot julienne
  • Fresh cucumber spears & cilantro
  • Mayonnaise or Maggi seasoning

Steps:

  1. Slice fresh baguettes open, toast crispy, and coat with mayonnaise.
  2. Pan-fry sliced firm tofu surfaces until golden brown.
  3. Layer hot tofu strips down across bottom bread halves.
  4. Stuff crispy quick-pickled daikon carrot root shreds into crumb spaces.
  5. Slide refreshing long cucumber spears and whole cilantro sprigs inside.
