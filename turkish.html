Lentil Soup

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup red lentils (rinsed thoroughly)
  • 1 medium onion & 1 carrot (diced)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp butter & 1 tsp dried mint
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (Pul Biber), lemon wedges

Steps:

  1. Sauté onion and carrot cubes in a large pot with oil until softened.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add rinsed red lentils and broth; bring to a boil, then turn down heat.
  4. Simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils break apart easily.
  5. Blend the soup matrix using an immersion blender until fully silky smooth.
  6. Melt butter with mint and pepper flakes, drizzle over bowls, and serve with lemon.

Doner Kebab

⏱️ 35 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g ground lamb or beef meat mixture
  • 1 small onion (grated and strained)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin, oregano, and paprika
  • Flatbread (Lavaş), sliced tomatoes, and onions
  • Garlic yogurt sauce wrapper spread

Steps:

  1. Knead ground meat with grated onion, minced garlic, and all spices until dense.
  2. Shape the meat into a tight log form, wrap in plastic, and freeze until solid.
  3. Shave paper-thin slices off the frozen meat log using a sharp knife.
  4. Flash-fry the thin shavings in a hot skillet with a little butter until crispy.
  5. Warm up lavaş flatbread pieces over the cooking meat juices.
  6. Roll up the crispy shaved doner meat inside bread with onions, tomato, and yogurt.
