Oka Ika

⏱️ 15 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Fresh yellowfin tuna fillets (cubed)
  • 1/2 cup Fresh lemon juice
  • 1.5 cups Coconut cream
  • 1 small Red onion (finely diced)
  • 1 Cucumber (diced)
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Dice your ultra-fresh fish into neat, bite-sized cubes.
  2. Pour the fresh lemon juice completely over the raw fish and marinate for 10-15 minutes until the outer edges turn a milky white.
  3. Drain away the excess lemon juice completely.
  4. Mix in your finely diced cucumber, red onions, and salt.
  5. Pour over the rich coconut cream, mix well, and leave in the fridge to chill thoroughly before serving cold.

Sapasui Veg

⏱️ 25 mins

Ingredients:

  • 250g Vermicelli glass noodles
  • 2 cups Mixed vegetables (carrots, cabbage, broccoli)
  • 1 Onion (sliced)
  • 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Grated ginger
  • 1/2 cup Dark soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil

Steps:

  1. Soak the vermicelli glass noodles in hot water for 10 minutes until soft, then drain and cut into manageable lengths.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or pan on medium-high heat.
  3. Sauté your sliced onions, garlic, and grated ginger until fragrant.
  4. Toss in the mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 4-5 minutes until crisp-tender.
  5. Add the noodles, pour dark soy sauce over everything, stir continuously until hot and fully combined, and serve warm.
