Boil Up

⏱️ 90 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg Pork bones (or bacon bones)
  • 500g Kumara (peeled and chopped)
  • 4 large Potatoes (peeled and quartered)
  • 1 bunch Puha (or watercress/sow thistle)
  • Doughboys (flour and water dumplings)
  • Water and Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Place pork bones in a large pot, cover with water, bring to a boil, and simmer for 1 to 1.5 hours until meat is tender.
  2. Skim any surface fat from the broth periodically.
  3. Add the potatoes and kumara to the pot.
  4. Prepare doughboys by mixing flour, a pinch of salt, and water into a firm dough, shaping into small balls, and dropping into the broth.
  5. Add the washed puha or watercress on top, cover tightly, and simmer for another 20 minutes until veggies and doughboys are completely cooked.

Rewena Bread

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup Rewena bug (traditional potato starter)
  • 5 cups Standard white flour
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 to 1.5 cups Warm water

Steps:

  1. Sift flour, salt, and sugar into a very large mixing bowl.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in your potato bug starter along with warm water.
  3. Mix thoroughly into a soft dough, transfer to a floured surface, and knead well for about 10–15 minutes.
  4. Shape into a loaf, place in a greased baking tin, cover, and leave to rise in a warm spot for 4 to 6 hours.
  5. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40 to 45 minutes until golden brown and it sounds hollow when tapped.
