Pad Thai

⏱️ 20 mins

Ingredients:

  • 150g flat rice noodles
  • 100g chicken breast strips
  • 2 tbsp tamarind pasta base
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce & brown sugar
  • Bean sprouts & chopped peanuts

Steps:

  1. Soak flat grain noodles inside lukewarm water blocks until pliable.
  2. Flash-fry chicken breast strips across broad oiled surfaces high.
  3. Push proteins aside, scramble fresh eggs directly onto hot centers.
  4. Incorporate drained noodle fibers, tamarind, and fish sauce.
  5. Throw green onion segments and fresh bean sprouts into the mix.
  6. Finish with crushed garden peanut toppings and fresh lime wedge juice.

Green Curry

⏱️ 20 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 200g chicken or bamboo shoots
  • Thai eggplants (quartered)
  • Fish sauce & palm sugar

Steps:

  1. Fry real green herbal pastes until intense aromatics break loose.
  2. Crack coconut cream directly into woks to split natural oils.
  3. Add white chicken slices, simmering into the emerging curry base.
  4. Incorporate crisp quartered round eggplants and bamboo shoots.
  5. Balance savory profiles using fish sauce drops and palm sugar crystals.
