Seafood Paella

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • 300g Paella Rice (Bomba)
  • 200g mixed seafood (shrimp, mussels, squid)
  • 2 cloves garlic (chopped) & 1 cup tomato purée
  • 700ml fish stock infused with saffron
  • 2 spoons olive oil, salt, and lemon wedges

Steps:

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide paella pan and gently sauté the garlic.
  2. Add the rice and tomato purée, stirring constantly for 2 minutes to coat the grains.
  3. Pour in the hot saffron stock. Simmer evenly for 15 minutes without stirring.
  4. Arrange the seafood on top and cook for another 8 minutes until stock is absorbed.

Tortilla Española

⏱️ 35 mins

Ingredients:

  • 4 large potatoes (peeled and thinly sliced)
  • 1 large onion (thinly sliced)
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup olive oil (for frying)
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Fry potatoes and onions in olive oil until soft but not browned; drain well.
  2. Beat the eggs in a large bowl, mix in the warm potatoes and onions, and salt well.
  3. Pour the mixture back into a hot oiled skillet on medium-low heat.
  4. Cook until sides set, flip carefully with a plate, and cook the reverse side until golden.
