Sushi

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • Nori sheets
  • Cucumber & avocado strips
  • Crab sticks or salmon

Steps:

  1. Steam sushi rice, dress with vinegar blends, and cool layout.
  2. Place a crisp sheet of nori flat onto rolling structures.
  3. Press seasoned sticky rice layers thin onto the sea vegetable bases.
  4. Line protein cores, avocado, and cool cucumber matchsticks across center.
  5. Roll together tightly using structural mats, then slice clean with wet steel knives.

Miso Soup

⏱️ 15 mins

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups dashi stock
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 100g silken tofu cubes
  • 2 tbsp dried wakame
  • Scallions

Steps:

  1. Bring rich dashi base infusions to small uniform boil rings.
  2. Hydrate deep-sea wakame structures directly inside hot bases.
  3. Drop fragile silken tofu block dice sets into pots carefully.
  4. Cut heat completely. Whisk raw live miso paste bases fluid smooth.
  5. Stir unified, then float garden onion arrays onto serving bowls.
