Lu Pulu

⏱️ 60 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 can Corned beef
  • 1 large Onion (sliced)
  • 2 Tomatoes (sliced, optional)
  • 1 can Thick coconut cream
  • 12-15 Fresh taro leaves (Lu leaves)
  • Aluminum foil for wrapping

Steps:

  1. Lay out a large square piece of aluminum foil and place 4-5 overlapping taro leaves in the center to form a sturdy pocket.
  2. Place a generous portion of canned corned beef, sliced onions, and chopped tomatoes right in the middle of the taro leaves.
  3. Carefully pour about 1/2 cup of thick coconut cream over the meat and fillings.
  4. Fold the taro leaves securely over the meat, then wrap the outer foil layer tightly to ensure no coconut cream leaks out.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for roughly 1 hour until the taro leaves inside are completely tender.

Poke Banana

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • 6 Ripe bananas (overripe works best)
  • 1 cup Arrowroot starch (or tapioca starch)
  • 1 can Thick coconut cream
  • 2 tbsp Sugar (optional)

Steps:

  1. Peel the ripe bananas and boil them in a large pot of water until they become soft and tender.
  2. Drain the water thoroughly and mash the hot bananas into a completely smooth paste inside a large mixing bowl.
  3. Gradually stir in the arrowroot starch, mixing continuously until it turns into a uniform, smooth consistency.
  4. Pour the banana mixture into a greased baking pan and bake at 180°C (350°F) for about 30–40 minutes until it sets firmly.
  5. Cut the baked pudding into cubes, place them into a serving dish, and pour warm, sweetened coconut cream over the top before serving.
