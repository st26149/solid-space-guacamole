Hummus

⏱️ 15 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 can chickpeas (drained, reserve liquid)
  • 1/3 cup tahini paste
  • 2-3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1-2 minced garlic cloves
  • Olive oil & cumin (to serve)

Steps:

  1. Process drained chickpeas and minced garlic cloves inside a food blender base uniformly.
  2. Pour rich sesame tahini paste, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt into the pureed blend.
  3. Stream reserved chickpea liquid slowly while blending until the structure hits a silky smooth texture.
  4. Spread the finished velvet cream into a shallow serving bowl, crafting structural rim ridges.
  5. Drizzle generous premium olive oil layers into the center and dust with cumin and paprika powder.

Shawarma

⏱️ 35 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken thighs (sliced thin)
  • 3 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp shawarma spice blend (cumin, coriander, allspice)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice & olive oil
  • Pita breads & garlic Toum sauce

Steps:

  1. Whisk yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, and complex aromatic spices in a wide marinating dish.
  2. Toss the sliced protein strips evenly through the sauce base and let rest for optimal flavor infusion.
  3. Sear the marinated chicken strips inside an intensely hot cast iron skillet until caramelized edges form.
  4. Warm individual soft flatbread rounds slightly over open flames or flat griddles.
  5. Coat flatbread layers heavily with white garlic Toum, adding savory spiced meat and pickled spears before rolling.
