Koshari

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup brown lentils & 1 cup white rice
  • 1 cup macaroni pasta
  • 1 can chickpeas (warmed)
  • 2 large onions (sliced into rings, fried crisp)
  • Spiced vinegar-garlic sauce (Dakka)
  • Spicy tomato sauce reduction

Steps:

  1. Boil brown lentils and white rice together with cumin until soft and fluffy.
  2. Cook the macaroni pasta separately in boiling water, drain well.
  3. Fry the sliced onion rings in hot oil until dark golden brown and super crunchy.
  4. Make a quick sauce of crushed tomatoes, vinegar, garlic, and cumin; simmer until thick.
  5. Layer the dish: rice and lentils first, then pasta, then warm whole chickpeas.
  6. Pour the tomato sauce over the top matrix and crown with piles of crispy fried onions.

Koshari Chicken

⏱️ 50 mins

Ingredients:

  • 300g chicken breast (cubed)
  • 1 cup brown lentils & 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 cup elbow pasta
  • 1 large onion (sliced thin and crispy-fried)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste mixed with garlic & cumin
  • 1 tbsp vinegar

Steps:

  1. Sauté chicken cubes in a pan with cumin, coriander, and oil until cooked through.
  2. Boil the lentils and rice in structured seasoned water blocks till tender.
  3. Prepare the pasta elements separately, drain, and set aside.
  4. Simmer tomato paste, garlic, vinegar, and chili powder together to make a rich, fiery sauce.
  5. Build plates by combining rice, lentils, pasta layers, and spiced chicken chunks.
  6. Finish with the tomato reduction sauce and rings of crunchy fried sweet onions.
