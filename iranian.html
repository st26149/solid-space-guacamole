Koobideh

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g ground beef or lamb (80% lean)
  • 1 large onion (grated, liquid fully squeezed out)
  • 1/2 tsp ground saffron (dissolved in 1 tbsp hot water)
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps:

  1. Mix the grated, drained onion with the ground meat in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add the salt, pepper, turmeric, and dissolved saffron liquid to the meat base.
  3. Knead the mixture thoroughly for 10 minutes until it becomes sticky and paste-like.
  4. Mold portions of meat around wide flat metal skewers, indenting with your fingers.
  5. Grill over hot charcoal or broil on high heat, turning frequently until fully cooked.
  6. Slide off the skewers gently and serve hot with buttered basmati rice.

Sabzi Polo

⏱️ 50 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice (washed and soaked)
  • 1 cup fresh dill (finely chopped)
  • 1 cup fresh parsley and cilantro (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 cup chives or green scallion tops (chopped)
  • 3 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 3 tbsp butter or oil, plus 1/4 tsp saffron

Steps:

  1. Parboil the soaked basmati rice in salted water for 6-8 minutes, then drain.
  2. Toss the drained rice lightly with all the chopped herbs and minced garlic.
  3. Add oil to the bottom of a heavy pot and layer sliced potatoes or flatbread for Tahdig.
  4. Mound the herbed rice mixture gently into the pot in a pyramid shape.
  5. Poke holes in the rice stack using a spoon handle to let steam rise freely.
  6. Cover tightly with a towel-wrapped lid and steam on low heat for 40 minutes.
