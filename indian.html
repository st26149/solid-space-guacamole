Butter Chicken

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • Chicken thighs (cubed)
  • Yogurt & garam masala
  • Tomato puree
  • Heavy cream & butter
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)

Steps:

  1. Marinate tender protein cuts completely inside spice-heavy yogurt mixes.
  2. Sear core ingredients golden over searing flame pans or skillet arrays.
  3. Simmer rich tomato reduction layers alongside heavy butter chunks.
  4. Pour rich dairy cream additions slow to yield smooth orange matrices.
  5. Stir marinated sears inside final gravies; dust with fragrant fenugreek leaf crushed arrays.

Butter Paneer

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 200g Paneer (cottage cheese cubes)
  • Cashew nuts (soaked and pasted)
  • Tomato-onion paste base
  • Garam masala & chili powder
  • Fresh cream & butter blocks

Steps:

  1. Pan-fry square paneer protein cubes until structural edges show gold.
  2. Blend soft-stewed onion-tomato mixes smooth along with cashew cream additions.
  3. Cook raw spice oils down inside melting real butter reservoirs.
  4. Unify cashew gravies with rich tomato base reductions on low heats.
  5. Submerge cheese blocks gentle, finish with fresh heavy cream pours.
