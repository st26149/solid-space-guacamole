Bobotie

⏱️ 50 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Minced beef or lamb
  • 1 large Onion (chopped) & 1 slice white bread
  • 1 cup Milk & 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp Curry powder & 1 tsp Turmeric
  • 2 tbsp Fruit chutney & 1 tbsp Raisins
  • Bay leaves for garnish

Steps:

  1. Soak your bread slice in a small bowl with half of the milk, then squeeze out excess milk (save the milk!).
  2. Sauté chopped onions, curry powder, and minced meat until brown. Mix in raisins, chutney, spices, and mashed soaked bread.
  3. Transfer spiced meat mixture into a greased baking pan, smoothing the top flat.
  4. Whisk remaining milk with eggs and a pinch of turmeric. Pour gently over the meat layer and place bay leaves on top.
  5. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30 minutes until the egg custard setup on top turns firm and golden.

Chakalaka

⏱️ 25 mins

Ingredients:

  • 3 Carrots (grated coarse)
  • 1 large Onion (sliced) & 1 Green bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 can Baked beans in tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder
  • 2 cloves Garlic & 1 fresh chili (minced)
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil

Steps:

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a pan and cook onions, green peppers, garlic, and chili until soft.
  2. Stir in the curry powder to coat the vegetables and fry for 1-2 minutes.
  3. Add grated carrots and mix well, allowing them to cook down slightly for 5 minutes.
  4. Tip in the canned baked beans along with all their tomato sauce base, stirring smoothly.
  5. Simmer together over medium-low heat for 10 minutes, season with salt, and serve hot or chilled.
