Fish & Chips

⏱️ 40 mins

Ingredients:

  • 4 thick white fish fillets (Cod or Haddock)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour & 1 cup cold beer
  • 1 small spoon baking powder
  • 4 large potatoes (cut into thick chips)
  • Vegetable oil (for frying) & salt

Steps:

  1. Parboil chips for 5 minutes, dry thoroughly, and fry in hot oil until pale golden.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and cold water/beer until a smooth batter forms.
  3. Dust fish in dry flour, submerge in batter, and deep fry until crispy gold.
  4. Re-fry chips for 2 minutes to achieve maximum crispiness. Serve everything hot.

Veggie Pie

⏱️ 45 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 pack pre-rolled puff pastry sheet
  • 2 potatoes (cubed) & 2 carrots (sliced)
  • 1 cup peas & 2 big spoons butter
  • 1 cup vegetable broth mixed with cream
  • 1 beaten egg (for glaze)

Steps:

  1. Melt butter in a pan and cook potatoes and carrots until soft.
  2. Stir in broth, cream, and peas, letting it simmer until thickened. Pour into dish.
  3. Drape the puff pastry sheet gently over the filling, crimping edges neatly.
  4. Brush with beaten egg glaze and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until puffed.
