Souvlaki

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g pork or chicken chunks
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced) & 1 spoon dried oregano
  • 3 spoons lemon juice & 2 spoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Whisk garlic, oregano, lemon juice, and olive oil in a bowl. Marinate meat for 1 hour.
  2. Thread marinated meat chunks firmly onto wooden skewers.
  3. Grill on a hot pan or barbecue grill over medium-high heat.
  4. Turn occasionally for 8–10 minutes until perfectly charred and cooked inside.

Greek Salad

⏱️ 15 mins

Ingredients:

  • 3 large tomatoes & 1 large cucumber (sliced)
  • 1 red onion (thinly sliced)
  • 150g Feta cheese blocks & ½ cup Kalamata olives
  • 3 spoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 small spoon dried oregano

Steps:

  1. Combine the tomatoes, cucumbers, and red onion slices in a large salad bowl.
  2. Scatter the rich Kalamata olives over the mixed vegetables.
  3. Place blocks of feta cheese right on top of the salad bed.
  4. Drizzle extra virgin olive oil and dust the surface with dried oregano before serving.
