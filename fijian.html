Kokoda

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g Fresh Walu (or mahi-mahi / snapper fillets)
  • 1/2 cup Fresh lime or lemon juice
  • 1 cup Thick coconut cream
  • 1 small Onion (finely minced)
  • 1 Red chili (finely sliced)
  • 1 Tomato (seeded and finely diced)
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Cut the fresh fish fillets into clean, uniform 1cm cubes.
  2. Place fish cubes in a glass bowl, pour the fresh lime juice over completely, and let marinate in the fridge for 2–3 hours until opaque.
  3. Drain away the excess lime juice thoroughly, squeezing the fish gently if necessary.
  4. Toss in the finely minced onion, sliced red chili, diced tomato, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Pour over the rich, chilled coconut cream, mix gently, and serve completely cold.

Rourou

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1 bunch Fresh taro leaves (Rourou leaves)
  • 1.5 cups Thick coconut cream
  • 1 small Onion (chopped)
  • 2 cloves Garlic (crushed)
  • Water for boiling & salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Wash taro leaves thoroughly, remove any thick fibrous stalks, and roughly chop the green leaves.
  2. Bring a pot of salted water to a rapid boil, add the chopped leaves, and cook uncovered for 10-15 minutes.
  3. Drain the boiling water completely from the cooked green leaves.
  4. In a separate clean pan, combine the cooked leaves with coconut cream, chopped onion, and crushed garlic.
  5. Simmer gently over low heat for an additional 10–15 minutes until rich, creamy, and thoroughly flavored.
