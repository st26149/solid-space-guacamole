Nyama Choma

⏱️ 90 mins

Ingredients:

  • 1kg Goat meat or Beef ribs
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 cloves Garlic & 1 inch Ginger (crushed, optional)
  • Warm water for basting

Steps:

  1. Preheat your charcoal grill or oven broiler to medium-high heat.
  2. Rub meat sections thoroughly with vegetable oil and season generously with coarse salt.
  3. Place the meat over the grill grates and let it brown slowly, turning occasionally.
  4. Baste periodically with salted water (mixed with garlic/ginger if preferred) to keep inside moist.
  5. Grill for 45-60 minutes until charred on the outside and tender. Carve into bite-sized strips and serve hot.

Ugali Veg

⏱️ 30 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups White cornmeal (maize flour)
  • 4 cups Water
  • 1 bunch Sukuma Wiki (collard greens, sliced thin)
  • 1 Tomato & 1 Onion (diced)
  • Salt and oil to taste

Steps:

  1. Bring 4 cups of water to a rolling boil in a pot. Slowly add cornmeal while stirring constantly with a wooden spoon.
  2. Press dough clumps against the sides of the pot as it thickens until it pulls away and forms a smooth, solid cake. Cover and let steam on low for 10 minutes.
  3. In a separate pan, sauté chopped onions and tomatoes in oil until broken down.
  4. Add the finely sliced collard greens and a pinch of salt. Toss quickly for 5 minutes until wilted but vibrant green.
  5. Turn out the hot Ugali cake onto a plate and serve alongside your fresh sautéed greens.
